# HTE-HSM Project

HTE-HSM is a web-based application designed to support the Mining Operations Department. The project was initiated to address inefficiencies in the **Pre-Start Inspection (P2H)** process, where manual checklists were frequently lost or unrecorded. As a result, critical issues identified in the field were not being escalated or addressed by the relevant departments.

To resolve these operational gaps, site management has initiated the development of an integrated data-center to track mining operations and ensure consistent issue follow-up.

---

## Project Stages

### 1. Pre-Start Inspection (P2H)

The primary objective of this stage is to capture high-quality, real-time data directly from the field (e.g., pits, hauling roads). This phase focuses on:

- **Collection:** Digitizing the P2H checklist process.
- **Recording:** Ensuring all inspection data is permanently stored.
- **Reporting:** Generating automated, actionable reports from field inputs.
- **Notification:** Broadcasting alerts to relevant departments when issues are flagged.
- **Oversight:** Enabling "bottom-to-top" monitoring to ensure accountability across all levels.

### 2. Operational Data Integration

This stage focuses on synthesizing field-level mining data—including equipment hour-meters, attendance lists, working hours, and personnel rosters—with **Human Capital** systems. This integration will provide a unified, accurate dataset, enabling data-driven decision-making regarding productivity and workforce management.

### 3. Mine Engineering Integration

This stage focuses on bridging the gap between field operations and long-term mine planning. By integrating real-time operational data with **Mine Engineering** datasets, we aim to close the loop between the "plan" and the "actual" site conditions. Key objectives include:

- **Haul Road Performance Analysis:** Comparing actual road condition reports (via P2H data) against design specifications to optimize cycle times and reduce maintenance frequency.
- **Production vs. Design Variance:** Correlating field-reported equipment hours and production logs with engineering block models to identify discrepancies in mining progress.
- **Feedback Loop:** Providing engineering teams with automated insights based on recurring field-level issues, allowing for proactive adjustments to mine design or operational procedures.
- **Technical Quality Control:** Ensuring that the technical standards established by the Engineering department are accurately translated into field tasks through digital instruction sets and monitoring.
